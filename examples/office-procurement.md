## RECOMMENDATION

### Primary Recommendation: Hybrid Approach

**Furniture:** Supplier B (CV Furnitura Jakarta)
- **Reason:** Best balance of price and spec compliance. Desk size difference (150×75 vs 140×70) is acceptable — actually provides more surface area. Local delivery, established store, reasonable warranty.
- **Action:** Negotiate delivery fee; confirm desk dimensions acceptable; request 12-month warranty extension.

**Electronics:** Supplier D (PT Tech Solutions Indonesia)
- **Reason:** Authorized brands, 3-year onsite warranty, setup included. Price premium justified by warranty and service.
- **Action:** Confirm specific models; negotiate volume discount (50 units); verify delivery timeline.

**Stationery:** Supplier F (Toko ATK Grosir)
- **Reason:** Reliable wholesaler, monthly standing order simplifies procurement, competitive pricing.
- **Action:** Set up monthly delivery schedule; negotiate annual contract discount.

### Why Not Supplier A (Furniture)?

Despite highest trust grade and exact spec match, price is Rp 36jt higher than Supplier B with no meaningful quality advantage for this use case. The 12-month warranty vs 6-month is nice but not worth 16% premium for a cost-conscious project.

### Why Not Supplier C (Furniture)?

Lowest price but:
- Chair lacks lumbar support (spec deviation)
- Whiteboard smaller than required
- 3-month warranty vs 6–12 months elsewhere
- Marketplace seller — harder to enforce warranty
- Shipping from Bandung adds lead time risk

### Why Not Marketplace for Electronics?

- Price range too wide (Rp 51jt spread)
- Quality/consistency varies by seller
- No setup service
- Warranty terms unclear
- For 50 units, dedicated reseller offers better support
