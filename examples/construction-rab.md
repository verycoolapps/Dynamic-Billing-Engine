## E. TECHNICAL ASSUMPTIONS

```
ASSUMPTION: Existing structure is sound; no structural reinforcement needed
ASSUMPTION: Floor levels adequate for new finishes; minor leveling only
ASSUMPTION: Electrical capacity sufficient for new load (panel upgrade included)
ASSUMPTION: Plumbing risers in good condition; fixture replacement only
ASSUMPTION: No hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint) present
ASSUMPTION: Building management provides after-hours access
ASSUMPTION: Work completed during dry season (Nov–Mar)
ASSUMPTION: Standard working hours 8h/day, 6 days/week
ASSUMPTION: Crew size 4–6 workers per activity
ASSUMPTION: All prices are ESTIMATED — no live market verification performed
ASSUMPTION: Prices based on Jakarta market rates as of Q4 2026 (illustrative)
ASSUMPTION: Wastage factors: concrete 3%, steel 5%, tile 8%, paint 10%
ASSUMPTION: PPN 11% applied to all taxable items
```

---

## F. EXCLUSIONS

```
- Structural reinforcement or foundation work
- Major MEP replacement (only fixture-level upgrades included)
- Furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E) — client provides
- IT infrastructure (network cabling, servers) — separate scope
- External facade work beyond signage
- Landscaping or exterior hardscape
- Permits and building approvals (assumed existing)
- Temporary relocation of occupants during work
- Post-handover maintenance or warranty service
- Inflation/escalation beyond 6-month validity period
```
