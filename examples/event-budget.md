## CONTINGENCY REASONING

Contingency set at **8%** of direct+indirect base because:

- **Venue uncertainty:** Final venue not confirmed — different venues have different requirements
- **Catering variability:** Headcount may fluctuate ±10%; per-person costs assumed mid-range
- **Production complexity:** Stage/AV setup has technical risk; backup equipment included
- **Weather/contingency:** Outdoor elements possible; indoor backup assumed but not guaranteed
- **Last-minute changes:** Corporate events often have scope changes

For comparison:
- **Simple event** (meeting, training): 3–5% contingency
- **Standard event** (gala, conference): 5–10% contingency
- **Complex event** (multi-day, outdoor, large-scale): 10–15% contingency

This event falls in "standard" category → 8% is reasonable.

```

- Elaborate thematic décor or installations

- Outdoor components (assumed indoor venue)
- Accommodation for attendees
```
