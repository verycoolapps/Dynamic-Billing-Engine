## RECOMMENDATION

### Primary: Supplier 2 — CV Listrik Grosir Sentosa

**Reason:** Best balance of price, delivery speed, and credibility. Rp 12jt cheaper than manufacturer with acceptable warranty. Local Jakarta delivery (2–3 days) critical for project timeline. Established physical presence allows site visits and relationship building.

**Action items:**
1. Request formal quote with exact brand specifications
2. Verify SNI certification on cable batches
3. Negotiate warranty extension to 12 months (match manufacturer)
4. Confirm delivery schedule and installation support
5. Establish Net 30 terms after first successful delivery

### Alternate: Supplier 1 — PT Kabelindo Listrik Tbk

**Reason:** Best quality assurance and warranty. If project requires strict SNI compliance or long-term reliability, manufacturer-direct is safest. Price premium (Rp 12jt) justified for critical infrastructure.

**When to choose alternate:**
- Project has zero tolerance for cable failures
- Warranty claims likely (harsh environment)
- Client requires manufacturer-direct sourcing
- Budget allows premium for peace of mind

### Excluded: Supplier 3 — Toko Online Listrik Jaya

**Reason:** Grade C trust, 3-month warranty insufficient for construction project, 7–10 day delivery too slow, 100% upfront payment risk. Price savings (Rp 12jt) not worth the risk for critical electrical infrastructure.

### Backup: Supplier 4 — PT Artha Listrik Nusantara

**Reason:** Viable if supply+install package needed. Clarify pricing structure first — may include installation markup.
